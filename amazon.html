01. The signals

What the algorithm reads — and what most setups miss.

Amazon's A9 algorithm ranks products, not ads. Sponsored placements are layered on top of the organic rank, not independent of it. The signals are documented from published sources and empirical testing.

01
Product data completeness.
Titles, bullet points, images, A+ content, attributes, backend search terms. The algorithm reads this before it reads the bid. Incomplete product data is the most common constraint we find. Every missing attribute is a signal gap that paid spend cannot close.
02
Review velocity and sentiment.
How quickly reviews accumulate and what they say. The algorithm weights recency and sentiment alongside volume. A product with 500 old reviews can be outperformed by a product with 50 recent positive ones. Velocity is the signal that most brands under-invest in.
03
Conversion rate by traffic source.
We track organic, sponsored and external conversion separately. A blended number hides whether the problem is product or placement. A high-spend product with low conversion may need page work, not a higher bid.
04
Price and promotion competitiveness.
Relative to the alternatives shown on the same search results page. We benchmark pricing, coupon depth, and deal timing against category competitors. The algorithm rewards the combination that converts, not the lowest price alone.
05
Inventory depth and fulfilment.
Stock-outs, shipping speed, and fulfilment method all feed the algorithm. A product that cannot ship Prime loses position even if everything else is improved. FBA vs FBA is increasingly a ranking signal, not just a logistics choice.
06
Paid position vs organic position.
We separate the two in every report. If position improves but conversion does not follow, we flag it rather than present it as success. Share of shelf is meaningless without the paid/organic split.
02. The ad products

Four surfaces. One campaign system.

Amazon offers four ad products, but they are only effective when planned and reported as one system. The bid on one product affects the rank of another. We structure them as a single campaign with shared objectives, not four separate budgets.

Sponsored Products
The foundation of every Amazon programme.
Keyword-targeted ads that appear in search results and product detail pages. We manage them against organic rank, not just ACOS. The goal is to earn the organic position through paid support, not replace organic revenue with paid spend.
Sponsored Brands
Brand awareness at the top of the funnel.
Customisable headline ads featuring your logo, tagline, and multiple products. Measured against category share of voice, not just click-through. The attribution window is longer than Sponsored Products and we track it separately.
Sponsored Display
Remarketing and audience targeting.
Display ads that follow shoppers across Amazon and off-platform. We use them for audience retargeting, cross-sell, and competitor conquest. The data feeds back into the product-level signal improvement loop.
Amazon DSP
Programmatic buying at scale.
Amazon's demand-side platform for buying display, video, and audio ads on and off Amazon. We use it to drive traffic into the algorithm, feed first-party data signals, and retarget across the post-purchase journey.
