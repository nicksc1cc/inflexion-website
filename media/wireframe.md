## Section 02 — The problem and the system

**Buyer question:** What problem do you solve and how?
**Intended understanding:** Separate channel reporting creates a fragmented view. Inflexion connects strategy, activation, commerce and measurement into one commercial framework.

**Tag:** The problem

**Heading (8 words):**
Separate reports. Separate channels. One commercial outcome.

**Copy (65 words):**
Most brands run media across search, social, programmatic, CTV, Amazon and retail media — each with its own dashboard, attribution window and definition of success. The reports are separate but the commercial outcome is not. Inflexion brings strategy, activation, commerce and measurement together around a single commercial objective, then measures what actually changed.

**Visual:** [Single integrated system diagram] — not two process flows. One connected visual showing: Strategy → Media + Commerce → Measurement → Intelligence → Investment. Annotations explain each stage briefly.

**Conclusion line (12 words):**
Every campaign creates intelligence. Every decision improves the next investment.

## Section 03 — Measurement and investment

**Buyer question:** Can you actually prove what works?
**Intended action:** Understand that Inflexion uses rigorous methods, not platform-reported ROAS.

**Tag:** Measurement

**Heading (6 words):**
Where should the next pound go?

**Copy (55 words):**
Platform-reported ROAS includes sales that would have happened anyway. Separating attribution from incrementality — understanding what media actually caused — requires experimentation, econometrics and commercial data, not a single dashboard number. Inflexion builds the measurement stack that answers: what changed, what caused it, and what should we do next.

**Visuals (3 exhibits in editorial gallery):**

**Exhibit A — What actually drove sales?**
Time-series: sales vs media activity, platform-attributed vs incremental contribution. Annotations at key points. Short finding: "Platform attribution overstates retail media contribution by an estimated 30–50% in this illustrative example."

**Exhibit B — Did the media cause the lift?**
Incrementality exhibit: treatment vs control, or modelled counterfactual showing pre-period, test period and estimated effect. "The estimated incremental effect is X sales. Without the experiment, this would appear as platform-reported ROAS."

**Exhibit C — Where should the next £1m go?**
Response curves, diminishing returns, reallocation scenario. Budget moved out equals budget moved in. "Shifting £500k from Channel A to Channel B increases estimated incremental contribution by £X."

**Conclusion line (9 words):**
Reported ROAS is not the same as commercial impact.

## Section 04 — Amazon and retail media

**Buyer question:** Do you genuinely understand Amazon and retail media?
**Intended understanding:** Inflexion treats Amazon as three integrated layers — media channel, commerce environment and signal source.

**Tag:** Amazon & retail media

**Heading (7 words):**
Amazon as media, commerce and signal.

**Copy (50 words):**
Amazon is the largest retail media surface and the most complex. Its value goes beyond campaign ROAS: the same platform generates advertising exposure, purchase transactions and customer intelligence. Understanding how these layers connect — and what they reveal about broader market dynamics — requires a joined-up analytical approach.

Amazon Ads · DSP · Sponsored Products, Brands, Display · Prime Video · Retail Media Networks · Retail Search · Off-site Audiences · New-to-Brand · Amazon Marketing Cloud

**Conclusion line (13 words):**
One commercial view across media, commerce and signal — not three separate dashboards.

## Section 05 — AI-powered intelligence

**Buyer question:** Do you use AI in a way that actually matters?
**Intended understanding:** AI accelerates analysis, identifies patterns and organises knowledge. It does not replace human judgement.

**Tag:** AI intelligence

**Heading (8 words):**
Faster analysis. Deeper patterns. Accumulating knowledge.

**Copy (55 words):**
AI allows us to analyse more market signals, monitor competitor activity at machine scale, model media scenarios and surface anomalies that would otherwise go unnoticed. But its real value is cumulative: every campaign, experiment and outcome feeds a shared knowledge model that makes the next analysis faster and more accurate. The system learns. People interpret.

**Visual:** [Exhibit E] — Single exhibit showing how market signals, competitor activity, campaign history and measurement findings contribute to a shared knowledge model. Concrete example: detected competitor change → hypothesis → proposed next action. "What the system found that the team had not noticed: Competitor A increased video spend 40% while shifting messaging from price to quality, coinciding with a 12% decline in your branded search conversion rate."

**No agent cards. No process lists. No separate automation section.**

**Conclusion line (11 words):**
AI gathers the evidence. People decide what it means.

## Section 06 — Human judgement

**Buyer question:** Why do I need you instead of the AI?
**Intended understanding:** The analytical layer is infrastructure. The judgement layer is the value.

**Tag:** Judgement

**Heading (4 words):**
Judgement changes everything.

**Copy (40 words):**
AI can analyse more, simulate more and surface more signals than any human team. But commercial decisions require context, experience, strategic instinct and the confidence to act on incomplete information. That is what experienced media and measurement specialists provide.

**Brand line (3 words):**
Less execution. More judgement.

**No cards, no grids, no list — a clean editorial closing statement.**
