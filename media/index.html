The problem

Media plans are siloed. The buyer journey isn't.

Most brands run retail media with one agency, Amazon with another, and programmatic with a third. Each reports their own ROAS. None sees the full picture.

The traffic you buy via programmatic feeds the retail algorithm. The product data you fix for Amazon improves your Walmart rank. The reviews you generate on Target become citation sources for AI answers. Splitting these across teams loses the compounding effects.

Integration is not a theoretical goal. It is the difference between knowing which combination of paid media, product data and retail signals actually moved the shelf, and knowing only what each channel reported independently.

How we work

One team, one measurement framework.

Diagnose

Full media audit: retail shelf position (paid + organic), Amazon signals, programmatic supply path, data quality, attribution gaps.

Unify

One measurement framework: share of shelf, organic vs paid, algorithmic signals, AI referral traffic, assisted conversions. Same data, same dashboard.

Deploy

Retail media + Amazon + programmatic run by the same team. Budget moves fluidly based on what moves the shelf, not what protects a silo's ROAS.

Refine

Weekly bid and budget shifts against shelf position. Monthly strategy review with full attribution rather than channel-level metrics alone.

Limitation: unification requires client willingness to share data across silos. We design the framework; the client must provide access.
