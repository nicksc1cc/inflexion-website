The problem

The customer sees one brand. Your media plan sees a dozen channels.

A customer does not experience search, social, Amazon, retail media, CTV and CRM as separate strategies. They experience one brand and one journey.

But agencies and platforms often organise media into separate channels, budgets, teams and reporting systems. The result is duplicated audiences, disconnected insight, competing objectives and a difficult question: where should the next pound go?

The plan fragments the journey. The customer does not.
