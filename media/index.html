The surface

Three buying environments. One commercial picture.

A programmatic impression sends a shopper to a retailer. Product data and reviews determine whether the visit converts. The resulting sales signals affect organic shelf position. Reporting each environment separately hides that chain. We connect them — not by flattening the measures, but by putting them in the same decision cycle.

Retail Media

Why most retail media investment is flying blind

Your ROAS numbers are probably lying to you.

If your retail media dashboard shows a blended ROAS of 6× but you cannot tell whether that includes organic sales the platform credited to itself, you have a measurement problem — not a performance problem. Platform-reported ROAS typically includes view-through attribution that credits organic purchases to the ad platform. The incremental-adjusted figure is closer to 2.4–4.3×. The gap is not small, and it matters when you are deciding whether to increase investment or redirect budget.

The same gap applies across most of retail media. More than 200 networks globally, each with its own attribution window, its own definition of a viewable impression, and its own methodology for crediting sales. The IAB Europe Attitudes to Retail Media Report (July 2025) found that 78% of buyers cite measurement as their top challenge, and 69% say attribution across networks is unresolved.

We do not claim to solve every measurement problem. We do insist on separating paid from organic share of shelf before any optimisation decision is made. A brand that sees 25% total share of voice but learns that 20% of it is paid and only 5% is organic is in a different position than one where the split is 12 / 13. Most dashboards do not show that distinction. Ours does.

AI efficiencies, not AI theatre

What AI actually changes in retail media.

Every agency now claims to be AI-powered. Most mean they have access to ChatGPT. Below is what we actually build, and the difference it makes to media performance.

Automated bid management across networks

Rule-based bid adjustments run against shelf position data, not just platform ROAS. When organic rank drops below a threshold on a high-margin SKU, we increase bid priority before the position is lost — not after the monthly report shows it happened.

Product signal health scoring

Before scaling ad spend on any SKU, we score its shelf readiness: content completeness, review velocity, stock cover, Buy Box stability, organic rank and margin after ad cost. SKUs below threshold get defensive spend only, with growth budget redirected to products that can convert profitably at higher volume.

AI discovery monitoring

AI-generated answers on search engines and retailer sites are becoming a primary discovery surface. We monitor how your brand appears in AI responses across retail and general search, then adjust content and signal strategy so the algorithmic shelf presents your products accurately.

Creative variant testing at scale

Retail media creative — product titles, images, A+ content, sponsored brand creative — is tested across networks using structured variant comparisons. We identify which creative elements drive conversion per network, and the winning variant goes live without waiting for a monthly creative review.

Cross-network budget allocation

When Amazon ACOS rises and Walmart Connect ROAS improves, we do not simply shift budget to the better number. We check organic rank movement, share of shelf trajectory and product signal health on both surfaces before reallocating. This prevents over-investing in a network where paid share is high but organic share is collapsing.

Measurement that separates paid from organic

Most AI media tools optimise against a blended ROAS target. Ours separates paid and organic share of shelf before any optimisation decision, then tracks both trajectories. A campaign that grows paid share but lets organic share decline is flagged for investigation, not reported as a win.
