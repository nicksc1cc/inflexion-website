Data + measurement underneath
Deep platform expertise. Independent thinking.

We know how the platforms work. We also know they don't see the whole picture.

Google, Meta, Amazon and retail networks are becoming more automated. The job is not to manually control every variable. It is to understand the signals, product data, creative, controls and economics that guide each system, then connect that expertise to the wider commercial decision.
