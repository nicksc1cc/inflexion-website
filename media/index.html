The problem

The reports are separate. The commercial outcome is not.

Retail media, Amazon and programmatic are often bought by different teams, each with its own dashboard and definition of success. That makes it difficult to tell whether a budget change improved the product, the placement or only the reporting.

The useful connection is practical. Programmatic can create demand that later appears on a retail shelf. Product content, availability and reviews affect conversion once the shopper arrives. Those signals should sit beside media performance, not in a separate conversation.

We do not promise that every channel can be reduced to one number. We keep the measures distinct, then put them in the same decision cycle: paid position, organic position, conversion, availability, spend and assisted demand.
