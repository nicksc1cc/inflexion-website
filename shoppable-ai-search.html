Shoppable AI search: the battle for share of generated sentence

Dunnhumby identifies the birth of shoppable AI search where brands battle for share of generated sentence. The focus is on summarising content for AI ingestion.

19 September 2026 · 8 min read · Inflexion journal
On this page01 / The shift from shelf to sentence02 / How AI search becomes shoppable03 / Content that gets ingested04 / Strategy for the generated sentence

For decades, retail media has been about the shelf. Physical shelf. Digital shelf. Algorithmic shelf. The unit of competition was placement. Position one. Endcap. Sponsored product slot.

Dunnhumby's 2025 retail tech trends identify a new unit: the generated sentence. In 2025, brands and retailers will start to battle for "share of generated sentence" as consumer adoption of AI search accelerates. The focus is on summarising content for AI ingestion and the recommendation mechanisms needed for content-rich product discovery.

This is not a metaphor. When a shopper asks an AI assistant "What running shoes work for wide feet?" the assistant generates a sentence: "For wide feet, the New Balance 1080v13 and Hoka Clifton 9 are frequently recommended for their wide toe boxes and accommodating fit." The brands mentioned in that sentence win the share of generated sentence. The brands not mentioned do not exist in that shopping moment.

01. The shift from shelf to sentence.

The shelf is a constrained physical or digital space. The generated sentence is an unconstrained semantic space. The shelf has finite slots. The sentence has finite attention. The competition has moved from placement to mention.

On Marketing's October 2025 survey found that 90 percent of people read Google AI Overviews when provided. More than half read them most of the time. The AI Overview is the new shelf. The generated sentence is the new placement.

HubSpot's State of AEO in 2026 report found that nearly half of surveyed marketers say they have personally made a business purchase decision based primarily on brand information they first discovered in AI-generated answers. AI referral traffic converts at 11.4 percent compared to 5.3 percent for organic search. Intent is the differentiator.

02. How AI search becomes shoppable.

Shoppable AI search operates at three layers:

Layer 1: Content ingestion. The AI system needs structured, extractable content. Product specifications, comparison tables, review summaries, use-case guides, FAQ pages. The content must be structured so the model can parse it, extract the answer, and cite the source.

Layer 2: Recommendation mechanisms. The model weights authority, relevance, clarity, and freshness. Authority comes from earned media citations, brand mentions across trusted sources, and entity recognition. Relevance comes from direct answers to specific questions. Clarity comes from extractable structure. Freshness comes from recent content updates.

Layer 3: Transaction enablement. Target, Etsy, Walmart, and Instacart have all enabled checkout in ChatGPT. One-click buy without leaving the chat. The generated sentence becomes the purchase trigger. "For wide feet, the New Balance 1080v13..." becomes a clickable link that completes the purchase in the chat interface.

The focus is on summarising content for AI ingestion and the recommendation mechanisms needed for content-rich product discovery. The adoption of AI search gives retailers a new way to capture digital customer journey signals.

03. Content that gets ingested.

Not all content is equal. The model prefers:

Comparison content. ChatGPT cites comparison content at a 95 percent rate. "New Balance 1080v13 vs Hoka Clifton 9 for wide feet" is the content the model wants.

Documentation and resources. ChatGPT cites documentation at 86 percent. Technical specifications, fit guides, use-case matrices.

Blog content. Google AI Overviews cite blog content at 42 percent. Perplexity cites blog posts and informative articles at 80 percent.

Pages with quotes or statistics. Pages with quotes or statistics have 30 to 40 percent higher visibility in AI answers.

Earned media. Muck Rack reports 84 percent of AI citations come from earned media. Owned content and paid sources account for smaller shares. Self-published content may provide less independent corroboration.

The content strategy shifts from keyword targeting to question answering. Map the questions shoppers ask. Create content that directly answers each question. Structure it for extraction. Distribute it to the sources the model trusts.

04. Strategy for the generated sentence.

The battle for share of generated sentence requires a different playbook:

Map the question set. Not keywords. Questions. "What running shoes work for wide feet?" "Which television works in bright rooms?" "What wireless earbuds suit Zoom calls?" Each question defines the content you need.

Build the answer library. One page per question. Direct answer in the first paragraph. Supporting evidence below. Structured for extraction: FAQ schema, comparison tables, specification lists, review summaries.

Earn the citations. The model cites what others say about you. Digital PR for AEO earns coverage in the sources models already cite. Source mapping, entity building, citation-weighted outreach. The source map is the targeting list.

Structure the entity. Wikipedia, Crunchbase, G2, industry directories, analyst listings. The sources models use to answer "who is company X." Audit every reference source. Fix inconsistencies. Build a complete entity footprint.

Activate community presence. Reddit, Quora, YouTube, Stack Exchange, niche forums. Community content is a growing share of AI citations. Answer real questions with natural language. The model reads the text around your brand name.

Track share of generated sentence. Not rankings. Not traffic. The proportion of a defined buyer prompt set, across a defined set of models, in which your brand appears in the generated sentence. Position within the sentence. Sentiment of the mention. Consistency across models.

The metric is fixed and published. The number means something because the questions did not change.
