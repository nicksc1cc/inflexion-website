01. What this means to you

The tools got cheap. Judgment didn't.

AI has pushed the cost of execution toward zero — media buying, content production, campaign build. What it can't do is decide which lever to pull, read the data correctly, or know when the model is wrong. That's what's scarce now. A small, senior team beats a large junior one.
