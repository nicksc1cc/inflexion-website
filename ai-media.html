The shift

Media buying is now autonomous. The question is what the algorithm prioritises.

In 2026, agentic AI does not just execute bids, it plans the media mix, allocates budget across channels, and adjusts in real time based on performance signals. The human role has shifted from "set the bid" to "design the objective function."

Most brands still run their media buying the same way they did five years ago: monthly budget meetings, static channel allocations, and ROAS reporting that arrives after the money is spent. The market has moved faster than the operating model.

01
Agentic media buying.
AI agents that perceive market conditions, reason about trade-offs, and execute across channels without human intervention for each decision. The buyer sets the strategy and the constraints; the agent works within them in real time.
02
First-party data activation.
With third-party cookies retired, the brands that win are the ones with clean, consented first-party data. We build the data layer, activate it across DSPs, and connect it to clean rooms for secure publisher collaboration.
03
Supply path improvement.
Not all programmatic inventory is equal. We audit the supply path, including the SSPs, exchanges and resellers your budget flows through, then identify layers that add cost without clear value. Fewer paths, higher quality, lower fees.
04
Cross-channel attribution.
The same data that feeds the algorithm also measures it. We build a unified attribution model that connects CTV exposures, display clicks, audio completions and DOOH footfall back to the same conversion event, so you can see which activity contributes to conversion.
The channels

Every surface, one signal layer.

Programmatic spans every screen. We plan them as one system, not a list of tactics.

Display
Programmatic display and native.
Open exchange, PMP, programmatic guaranteed. Contextual targeting powered by AI, not keyword lists. We aim for attention signals and viewability, not just impression volume.
Connected TV
Streaming television with digital targeting.
Programmatic CTV inventory growing 26% year-over-year. Ad-supported tiers on Netflix, Disney+, and the major streamers. Interactive and shoppable formats. We bring digital precision to television's sight-and-sound impact.
Streaming Audio
Spotify, Pandora, podcasts.
Programmatic audio ads with frequency capping, audience targeting, and creative rotation. Audio is a growing share of the programmatic mix, especially for commuter and in-home listening environments.
Digital Out-of-Home
Programmatic DOOH buying.
Digital billboards, street furniture and transit advertising are now bought programmatically with audience targeting, dayparting and creative rotation. DOOH combines digital targeting with physical placements.
The data layer

Clean data is the competitive advantage.

AI-powered media buying is only as good as the data feeding it. Most brands have seven disconnected tools producing conflicting signals. The first deliverable on every engagement is a clean, unified data layer that every channel reads from.

01
First-party data audit.
Audit what data you have, where it lives, and whether it is consented. Build the clean room strategy for secure publisher collaboration.
02
Signal architecture.
Define the signals the algorithm should improve: not just ROAS, but share of shelf, citation velocity, AI referral traffic and brand search lift.
03
Measurement framework.
One attribution model across every channel. Same conversion event, same window, same reporting. Decisions built on data that is trusted because it is consistent.
The supply chain

Where the money goes, and what stays.

Programmatic media flows through multiple intermediaries before reaching a publisher. Each layer adds cost and, in some cases, data leakage. We audit the supply path to identify SSP fees, reseller markups and hidden exchange costs that reduce effective CPM. In our experience, between 20% and 40% of programmatic budget can be lost to supply path inefficiency.
