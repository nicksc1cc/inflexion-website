AEO: the shift from keyword rankings to citation tracking

Search is no longer about ranking for keywords. It is about being cited in AI answers. Here is how the measurement model is changing.

19 September 2026 · 10 min read · Inflexion journal
On this page01 / The old model02 / The new model03 / What measurement looks like now04 / Different engines, different signals05 / What this means for your strategy

For two decades, the SEO playbook was stable. Rank for keywords. Drive traffic. Convert visitors. The metric that mattered was position one through ten.

That model is breaking. Not because SEO stopped working. Because the surface where discovery happens has shifted.

Semrush projects that LLM referral traffic will overtake traditional Google search by the end of 2027. In the past three months alone, LLM referrals grew 800 percent year over year. Google AI Overviews now appear on billions of searches monthly, covering at least 13 percent of all SERPs. The surface has changed. The measurement has not caught up.

01. The old model was built for clicks. The new model is built for citations.

The old question: where do we rank for this keyword? The new question: does the model cite us when someone asks this question?

They are not the same. A page ranking position three for a keyword may be absent from the AI answer to the same query. We have measured this gap across dozens of categories. It is larger than most marketing teams realise. AI Overviews and chatbots do not simply read the top ten results. They retrieve from a wider index, weight authority differently, and prefer content structured for direct extraction.

The metric that matters is not position. It is share of answer. How often does your brand appear in the generated response? At what position within the answer? With what sentiment?

Ranking is a proxy for visibility. Citation is visibility itself.

02. The signals have changed. The playbook has not caught up.

Traditional SEO optimises for keyword density, backlink volume, page speed, and technical crawlability. AEO optimises for different signals.

Authority now means how often other trusted sources cite you. Backlinks still matter. But unlinked brand mentions across Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube, industry forums, and news outlets now carry weight. LLMs read the text around your brand name. They do not require a hyperlink to register the association.

Relevance means whether your content directly answers the question being asked. Keyword density matters less than factual clarity. A clear, direct answer in the first paragraph of a page outweighs keyword-stuffed content that buries the answer.

Clarity means whether the model can parse your content. Structure matters. Schema markup, FAQ format, clear headings, and extractable answers help the model retrieve and cite you. Pages with quotes or statistics have been shown to have 30 to 40 percent higher visibility in AI answers.

Freshness means how recent your content is. LLMs have a clear preference for recent content. Content audits are non-negotiable. Update statistics, screenshots, and examples. Remove references to outdated tools. Add new sections covering recent developments.

The old playbook: keyword density, backlink volume, page speed. The new playbook: authority mentions, direct answers, extractable structure, content freshness.

03. Measurement has moved from rankings to share of answer.

You cannot optimise what you do not measure. The old dashboard showed keyword rankings, organic traffic, and click-through rate. The new dashboard needs different metrics.

Share of answer: the proportion of a defined buyer prompt set, across a defined set of models, in which your brand appears in the generated answer. Position within the answer is recorded. Being named as the first recommendation is different from being mentioned in a list of three.

Citation sources: when a model names a competitor, trace what it drew from. Independent reviews, comparison tables, analyst reports, forum discussions. The source map tells you exactly where to build credibility.

Sentiment and framing: is your brand recommended positively, mentioned neutrally, or described with caveats? Models do not express opinions, but their wording frames brands differently. A brand described as "a leading provider" competes differently from one described as "also offers."

Cross-model consistency: models disagree. A brand well-cited in Claude can be absent from GPT. The 11 percent domain overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity proves it. You need visibility across the models your buyers actually use.

The prompt set is fixed and published. The number means something because the questions did not change.

04. Different engines, different signals. One strategy does not transfer.

ChatGPT favors credible, authoritative sources with clear entity signals. Comparison content has a 95 percent citation rate. Documentation and resources hit 86 percent.

Google Gemini pulls heavily from pages that already perform well organically, typically within the top 20 results. Blog content performs particularly well in AI Overviews at 42 percent citation rate.

Perplexity cites well-researched, comprehensive content that combines multiple perspectives. Academic sources and data-driven content perform well. Blog posts and informative articles hit 80 percent citation rate.

Copilot favors content that clearly states what a product or service does, includes pricing or comparison information, and has strong brand signals.

The strategy shifts by target engine. A single approach does not transfer. You need to know which engines your buyers use and optimise for their specific retrieval logic.

05. What this means for your strategy.

The shift is not optional. On Marketing's October 2025 survey found that 37 percent of marketing teams are already actively optimising for AI search. Another 30 percent are exploring it. Eighty percent plan to have AI search optimisation strategies in place within a year.

The teams that start tracking now will have the baseline when the market catches up. The teams that wait will be measuring from zero.

Start with a pilot. Map 5 to 10 high-priority buyer questions. Test across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Record where you appear, where competitors appear, and where nobody does. That baseline document is yours to keep.

Then build the system: prompt mapping, citation tracking, source authority mapping, content built for citation. Track monthly against the fixed prompt set. The number means something because the questions did not change.

AEO does not replace SEO. Strong SEO fuels AEO. AI systems favour content from trusted domains with strong organic rankings. But the measurement model has shifted. The teams that understand the new metrics will make better decisions. The teams that keep reporting keyword rankings will be reporting on a surface that matters less every quarter.
