01.The problem

Your own website drives a fraction of AI citations.

When an AI model answers a question about your category, it reaches for sources it already trusts — not your homepage. Studies show that 82% of citations in AI-generated answers come from earned media: Wikipedia, Reddit, Quora, YouTube, G2, Trustpilot, analyst reports, and trusted trade publications. Your brand's own website accounts for just 5–10%.

This is not an SEO problem. It is an authority problem. AI models build confidence in a source by seeing it referenced across multiple high-authority references. A brand that exists only on its own domain does not generate the cross-source citation density that signals credibility to a model.

More telling: only 2% of journalists targeted by traditional PR pitching overlap with the journalists and outlets that AI models actually cite. The standard PR playbook — blast a press release to a media list — hits the wrong targets almost every time.

82% vs 5–10%
AI models cite earned media 8–16× more often than a brand's own website. Investing in your owned content alone cannot close the gap.
02.How it works

Build the citation footprint models recognise.

Every piece of coverage is selected for its weight in the model's citation graph — not for its domain authority or PageRank pass-through.

Phase 1
Source mapping
Every category has a different citation graph. We map which outlets, forums, research papers, and data sets carry weight with AI models for your specific market. The source map becomes the targeting list for every piece of outreach. Distribution across these sources increases citations by 325%.
Phase 2
Entity building
Wikipedia, Crunchbase, G2, industry association directories — these are the sources models use to answer "who is company X" and "what do they do." We build and maintain your entry in every relevant reference source so the model has clear entity signals.
Phase 3
Earned coverage
We target the specific outlets that models cite most in your category — not the outlets in a generic media database. A mention in a specialist trade publication that AI models cite 40× more often is worth ten generic mentions.
Phase 4
Community presence
Reddit, Quora, YouTube, Stack Exchange, niche forums — community-generated content is a growing share of AI training data. We build a structured presence in the platforms models cite most, answering the questions your buyers ask in natural language.
03.Why it is different

This is not PR. It is citation engineering.

Old
Links for rankings.
Traditional digital PR measures success by the number and quality of backlinks earned. The goal is PageRank. The metric is domain authority. The audience is the Google crawler.
New
Citations for AI visibility.
AI citation PR measures whether your brand appears in the generated answer — and how often. A mention in a trusted source without a link can still influence the model's output. The audience is the model.
Shift
Links are a means, not the end.
An unlinked mention in a high-authority outlet can be more valuable for AI visibility than a link from a low-quality one. We still convert unlinked mentions, but the primary metric is Share of Citation across a fixed prompt set — not backlinks.
Fact
2% overlap problem.
Only 2% of journalists on standard PR pitching lists are the same journalists whose outlets AI models cite. The other 98% is noise. AI citation PR starts from the model's source map, not a media database subscription.

05.How we measure

Share of citation, tracked back to specific coverage.

Our measurement rig runs the same buyer prompts across 4–6 models, every month. We attribute every shift in citation volume back to a piece of coverage.

Baseline
Fixed prompt set, multi-model audit
Before any PR activity, we run 30–50 buyer-aligned prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot. This establishes your Share of Citation and reveals which sources drive competitor citations.
Track
Monthly sentiment & source distribution
Coverage volume alone is misleading if sentiment is neutral or absent. We track whether citations carry positive, neutral, or negative positioning — and which source categories (news, forums, review sites, analyst reports) contribute most to your footprint.
Attribute
Coverage → citation movement
Every time a piece of coverage lands, we rerun the prompt set. Over 4–6 months, we build a signal model that tells you exactly which types of coverage actually move AI visibility in your category — and which are noise.

06.What we won't tell you it does

The honest limitations, up front.

01
Coverage alone is not enough.
A single high-authority mention will not transform your AI visibility if the rest of your citation footprint is empty. This is a compounding strategy, not a quick fix. Expect meaningful movement at month three, not week one.
02
Models do not re-crawl in real time.
A mention published today may take weeks or months to appear in model answers — if it appears at all. The model's training cut-off, retrieval schedule, and ranking algorithm all affect whether a new source gets cited. We set expectations at month three.
03
It cannot fix a weak underlying narrative.
If your product, positioning, or differentiation is unclear, more coverage amplifies a confused message. The PR campaign must be built on a foundation the market understands — that is a separate deliverable, and we will tell you if it is missing.
04
The 2% overlap cuts both ways.
The journalists and outlets that AI models cite are a narrow, stable set. Breaking into that set is hard. Existing relationships with traditional PR contacts rarely translate. We are honest about that from the outset.
