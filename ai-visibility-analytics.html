01. The problem

You cannot optimise what you do not measure.

Most brands have no baseline for their AI visibility. They know what their Google rankings look like. They know their Amazon share of voice. But they have no picture of how often they appear in AI-generated answers — across which models, in response to which buyer questions, and against which competitors.

of brands stay visible across consecutive AI sessions. The other 70% appear in one answer and vanish in the next. A baseline is the only way to tell if you are gaining or losing ground. Source: AirOps, 2026 State of AI Search

The gap between search and answer is real: brands ranking first on Google are entirely absent from the AI answer to the same query. We have measured it. Without measurement, the work is guesswork. The analytics are the diagnostic that everything else depends on.

Citation intelligence

Reddit, Wikipedia, Quora, YouTube, analyst reports, niche forums — the source map reveals exactly where competitors earn citations you do not, informing your content and PR strategies. 82% of AI citations come from earned media, not owned content. We map where yours land.
Sentiment analysis
Monitor how AI platforms position your brand versus competitors.
Track sentiment patterns across different models and topics. Are you recommended positively? Mentioned neutrally? Absent? The sentiment picture tells you whether your brand narrative is landing where it matters — and which competitors are winning the positioning battle in AI answers.
03. Competitive intelligence

Know where you stand against every competitor.

SOV
Share of voice across every model.
See how frequently you appear versus competitors across different AI platforms and product categories. Track which specific models and topics your competitors dominate. A brand with 40% share on ChatGPT may have 0% on Perplexity — the 11% domain overlap between the two makes SOV per-model the only meaningful metric.
Gap
Competitive citation gap analysis.
Identify exactly where you are missing citations compared to each competitor. See every source they draw from that you do not — and the ones that neither of you owns yet. These gaps are your highest-leverage content and PR opportunities, ranked by citation frequency across models.
Benchmark
Category performance benchmarking.
Compare your AI visibility against industry benchmarks. Understand whether your category is under-optimised (most are) and where the quick wins sit. Only 30% of brands maintain consistent visibility across sessions. If you are above baseline, that is a defensible moat — and if not, the path is measurable.
