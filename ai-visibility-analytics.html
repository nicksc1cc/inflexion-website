01. The problem

You cannot optimise what you do not measure.

Most brands have no baseline for their AI visibility. They know what their Google rankings look like. They know their Amazon share of voice. But they have no picture of how often they appear in AI-generated answers — across which models, in response to which buyer questions, and against which competitors.

30%
of brands stay visible across consecutive AI sessions. The other 70% are not consistently visible across sessions. A baseline is the only way to tell if you are gaining or losing ground. Source: AirOps, 2026 State of AI Search

The gap between search and answer is real: brands ranking first on Google are entirely absent from the AI answer to the same query. We have measured it. Without measurement, the work is guesswork. The analytics provide the baseline for the work that follows.

Multi-model tracking
Monitor brand presence across every major AI platform.
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Claude, Amazon Rufus, Walmart Sparky, Grok, and more. Models disagree more than expected; a brand well-cited in Claude can be entirely absent from GPT. The reported 11% domain overlap illustrates the difference. We track multiple platforms to show how results vary between them.
Prompt-level analysis
See the exact prompts and responses behind every mention.
Understand not just how often you appear, but how you are positioned versus competitors across different buyer scenarios: from broad awareness queries like "best [category]" to comparison queries like "[brand] vs [brand]" and purchase-intent questions. Every response is captured and categorised.
Citation intelligence
Discover which sources influence AI recommendations in your category.
Reddit, Wikipedia, Quora, YouTube, analyst reports, niche forums: the source map shows where competitors earn citations that you do not, informing your content and PR strategies. One cited study reports that 82% of AI citations came from earned media, not owned content. We map where yours land.
Sentiment analysis
Monitor how AI platforms position your brand versus competitors.
Track sentiment patterns across different models and topics. Are you recommended positively? Mentioned neutrally? Absent? The sentiment picture tells you whether your brand narrative is landing where it matters, and which competitors are more positively positioned in AI answers.

SOV
Share of voice across every model.
See how frequently you appear versus competitors across different AI platforms and product categories. Track which specific models and topics your competitors dominate. A brand with 40% share on ChatGPT may have 0% on Perplexity. The 11% domain overlap between the two makes SOV per-model the only meaningful metric.
Gap
Competitive citation gap analysis.
Identify exactly where you are missing citations compared to each competitor. See every source they draw from that you do not, and the ones that neither of you owns yet. These gaps can help prioritise content and PR opportunities, ranked by citation frequency across models.
Benchmark
Category performance benchmarking.
Compare your AI visibility against industry benchmarks. Understand whether your category is under-improved (most are) and where the quick wins sit. Only 30% of brands maintain consistent visibility across sessions. That may provide an advantage. If not, the gap can still be tracked over time.
