01 / The shift

AI makes execution cheap. Value moves to what platforms can't do.

01

Platforms take execution

Google AI Max, Meta's automated ads, Amazon Ads Agent and Klaviyo Composer all launched or expanded in the past year. Buying, segmentation and campaign builds are moving inside the tools.

02

Brands must be legible to machines

AI answers, shopping agents and B2B buying agents all read structured, cited information. Being found is no longer enough. Brands need to be understood and chosen.

03

Judgement earns the fee

When the work is cheap, clients pay for the call on what to do. Every platform now grades its own AI campaigns, so an independent answer is worth more.

The lens follows Sequoia's Services: The New Software: AI first service companies start where work is already outsourced and mostly intelligence, then move into judgement as their data compounds.

07 / Method and sources

How the map was built.

We started from what providers actually sell. For each of 13 disciplines we inventoried the service lines offered by holding companies, independents, specialists, consultancies, platforms and AI-native entrants, with published pricing and new service lines from the last 18 months. We then added 14 services AI is creating, each backed by a dated 2026 signal.

Each service is placed by two judgements: the share of the work that is judgement and the share already bought from outside. Dot area and bar length show relative size: an Inflexion estimate built from published category totals (the agency, SEO, research, PR and influencer markets) and allocated across service lines. We show it as an index, not dollars, because the allocation is a judgement, not a measurement. Platform risk rates how directly Google, Meta, Amazon or another platform is automating the task inside its own product. Moat rates whether doing the work builds data that improves the next job. New services carry a horizon instead of a size.

Judgement shares are weighted by relative size: each service's size multiplied by its judgement share. Scores are analytical judgements from the evidence. Twenty services were checked against at least two provider service pages or pricing sources each.

Where the money is going lists funding rounds, acquisitions and platform launches from January 2025 to October 2026, taken from company announcements and trade press. Each event links to its source.
