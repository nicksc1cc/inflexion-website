The shift

Marketing was built for a different economics.

When execution was expensive, teams organised around channels, agencies, specialists and campaign cycles. Each part of the operation ran separately because coordination cost more than isolation.

Now AI can analyse, monitor, create, test, model, report, recommend and execute. The economics inverted. The scarce resource is no longer execution capacity — it is judgement about what matters.

The organisation cannot simply add AI to the existing structure. It has to reconsider the structure itself.
