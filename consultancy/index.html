01

Marketing was built for a different economics.

Then

Execution was expensive. Teams were built around channels, agencies, specialists, manual analysis, campaign cycles and reporting.

Now

AI can analyse, monitor, create, test, model, report, recommend and execute. The organisation must reconsider its structure, not just add AI to it.

The organisation cannot simply add AI to the existing structure. It has to reconsider the structure itself.
