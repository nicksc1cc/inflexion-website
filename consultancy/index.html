The problem

Marketing has become harder to run.

More agencies. More platforms. More data. More channels. More people involved in the same decision.

Then AI arrives and changes what can be automated, what teams need to do and where expertise sits.

Too much work

The approach

We start with how the work actually happens.

We look at the people involved, the agencies they work with, the processes they follow, the technology they use and the decisions they need to make.

Then we work out what should stay, what should change and what should disappear.

We can work with an existing team and agency structure, alongside it, or independently on a specific project.
