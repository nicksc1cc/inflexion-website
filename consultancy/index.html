01 / The economic shift

The organisation was built around expensive execution.

Marketing was organised around channels, specialists, agencies and campaign cycles because execution required people.

AI changes the economics of much of that work.

02 / The pressure test

We start with the work, not the technology.

Before recommending a platform, an agency, an AI tool or an organisational change, we examine how the work actually gets done.

01
The work
What are people actually spending their time doing?

Separate high-value judgement from research, reporting, administration, production and repetitive execution.

02
The knowledge
Where does the organisation’s knowledge live?

Identify information trapped across teams, agencies, platforms, spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

03
The decisions
Where are decisions actually being made?

Identify decisions that are slow, duplicated, poorly informed or unnecessarily dependent on manual work.

04
The agencies
What should an agency still be doing?

Separate genuinely differentiated expertise from execution that can increasingly happen through internal capability, technology or automation.

05
The system
What should be connected, automated or intelligent?

Identify where data, AI and automation can remove friction and improve the speed or quality of decisions.

06
The judgement
Where does a person still need to decide?

Strategy. Context. Commercial trade-offs. Creativity. Risk. Brand. Accountability.

The Inflexion principle
Less execution. More judgement.
