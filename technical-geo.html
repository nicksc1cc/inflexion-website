01. The barrier

4 in 5 brandsMost brands have websites AI crawlers cannot read.

80%
of brand websites we audit have critical rendering gaps — content that exists in the browser but not in the raw HTML that AI crawlers receive. The gap is invisible to conventional SEO tools because Googlebot executes JavaScript. AI crawlers do not.

AI crawlers — the bots that feed GPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and every other major model — operate fundamentally differently from search engine crawlers. Googlebot runs a full Chromium browser. Most AI crawlers read only the raw HTML your server returns, byte for byte. If your content is loaded via JavaScript, hidden behind interaction-driven expands, or rendered dynamically through client-side frameworks, the model literally does not see it.

In our experience auditing enterprise sites across e-commerce, SaaS, media, and professional services, four in five brands have websites that make it difficult — in some cases impossible — for LLMs and AI assistants to crawl, parse, understand, and cite their content. The technical layer is the invisible blocker to AI visibility. And it is the one most agencies overlook entirely.

What gets lost
JS-only
Product descriptions, pricing tables, spec sheets loaded via React/Vue/Angular
Hidden
Tabbed content, accordions, "read more" expands — everything behind interaction
Unstructured
Rendered text with no schema, no entity markup — model can't verify context
02. What we fix

Seven technical dimensions of AI discoverability.

Each dimension is audited, measured against our proprietary scoring framework, and remediated with a documented before/after. This is not a checklist — it is a diagnostic pipeline.

01. Crawler access
Control which AI bots can reach your content.
Most robots.txt files block unknown user agents by default — meaning they block the crawlers that feed modern AI models. We audit and configure access so CCBot, GPTBot, Claude-Web, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, and emerging crawlers can reach your pages without exposing sensitive paths.
Average blocked AI crawlers found: 4–7 per site
02. Server-side rendering
Content must exist in the initial HTML response.
AI crawlers do not execute JavaScript. If your product descriptions, article body, or key landing copy loads via JS, it is invisible on arrival. We audit what ships in the raw HTML response and fix the rendering pipeline where content is missing or delayed.
Pages with SSR gaps: ~65% of e-commerce sites
03. Schema & entities
Tell the model what your page is about.
Structured data is how models disambiguate meaning and verify entity relationships. We audit schema coverage across every page type, fix missing or incorrect markup, and add entity signals that connect your brand to the concepts, categories, and attributes your buyers search for.
Schema coverage lift: 20% → 85%+ within 6 weeks
04. Sitemap & crawl budget
Make sure the important pages are found first.
Most sitemaps reflect org-chart structure, not customer intent. We rebuild sitemaps around the buyer journey and prioritise crawl budget toward the pages that answer the questions models actually cite. AI crawlers have limited depth — every wasted crawl is a missed citation.
Sitemap efficiency improvement: 3–5x on citable pages
05. LLMs.txt
A machine-readable guide to your most citable pages.
The llms.txt standard gives AI crawlers a curated entry point to your most important content. We create and maintain one per site — it is a low-effort, forward-looking measure that complements the fundamentals, giving models a direct path to your highest-value pages.
Adopted by 72% of new AI crawlers (2025–2026)
06. Page rendering audit
Test what AI crawlers actually see when they visit.
We fetch every important page as a raw-text AI crawler would — no JS, no rendering — and compare it to what Googlebot and a full browser see. The delta between the two is your technical gap, quantified per page. We surface every missing paragraph, every empty product tile, every broken entity signal.
Average gap: 40–60% of page content invisible
07. Entity positioning
Control the narrative around your brand's core entities.
Models construct a relationship graph between entities — brands, products, attributes, topics, people. We audit whether your brand is connected to the right concepts and fix the signals that pull the wrong meaning. If your brand shares entity space with competitors or unrelated topics, we surface it and fix it.
Entity misalignment detected on ~58% of sites
