01.The gap

The ambition is there. The architecture isn't.

92% of marketing leaders say they plan to rewire their teams for AI — but fewer than half have started. The gap is not willingness. It is knowing what to do, in what order, and how to make it stick.

The problem is structural. Marketing teams were built for a world where discovery, media, and the shelf were separate functions with separate budgets, separate agencies, and separate data sets. AI reads them all as one signal. The operating model has not caught up.

We help close that gap. Not by selling you a playbook, but by building the data infrastructure, the AI deployment plan, the automation roadmap, and the team structure to run it — in that order.

02.What we do

Four capabilities. One system.

We combine data strategy, AI deployment, marketing automation, and operating model design into a single programme. Each feeds the next.

I. Data strategy
Connect the data that connects the channels.
Most brands have discovery data here, retail media data there, and CRM data somewhere else. We build the unified data layer that lets you see the full picture — and measure what actually moves the business.
II. AI strategy
Deploy AI where it moves the business.
Not AI for the sake of it. We assess where AI can meaningfully improve output — citation optimisation, content generation, media allocation, customer insight — and build the deployment plan that matches your maturity level.
III. Marketing automation
Automate what shouldn't be manual.
Reporting, content distribution, bid management, creative personalisation, workflow orchestration. We identify the processes that consume team time without adding value, and build the automation that frees your best people for the work that matters.
IV. Operating model
Restructure the team to run it.
The technology is the easy part. The hard part is reorganising the team around the new model. We design the structure, the roles, the processes, and the governance that make the new operating model sustainable — not dependent on the people who built it.
03.Maturity model

Three stages. Most teams are in stage one.

We assess where you sit on the maturity curve, then build the roadmap to the next stage. The goal is not to leap to stage three overnight. It is to make consistent progress that compounds.

A
Siloed & manual
Separate teams, separate data, no automation
B
Coordinated & measured
Shared data, some automation, aligned KPIs
C
Unified & autonomous
One team, one data layer, AI-driven operations
A
Siloed and manual.
Discovery, media, and the shelf are run by different teams with different budgets and different data. Reporting is manual. Decisions are made on lagging indicators. Automation is limited to basic bid management. Most brands are here.
B
Coordinated and measured.
Data is shared across functions. Common KPIs exist. Automation handles routine reporting and optimisation. The team is beginning to connect discovery and shelf signals. AI is used for content generation and basic analysis.
C
Unified and autonomous.
One team runs discovery and the shelf with a single data layer. AI drives content creation, media allocation, citation optimisation, and personalisation. The team focuses on strategy, exception handling, and continuous improvement. The operating model is sustainable.

06.What we won't tell you

The honest limitations, up front.

01
This is not a quick fix.
Transforming how a team operates takes months, not weeks. If you need a tactical fix by Friday, this is not the engagement.
02
Technology alone does not change anything.
Buying a platform and not changing the team structure is the most expensive way to stay where you are. We design the operating model first, then select the technology.
03
You will need to make hard decisions.
Restructuring the team means changing roles, reassigning people, and potentially retiring processes that have been in place for years. We will tell you what needs to change. You have to execute it.
04
We do not run your agency day-to-day.
We design the model, build the infrastructure, and advise the leadership. If you need someone to manage the day-to-day execution, we will recommend partners — but we do not compete with agencies.

You know what needs to change. We can show you how.
