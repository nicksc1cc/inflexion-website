01. The problem

Search became answers. Your SEO didn't follow.

Answer Engine Optimisation is not rebadged SEO. The signals are fundamentally different: keyword density matters less than factual clarity; backlink volume matters less than citation frequency across authoritative sources; page speed matters less than whether your content can be parsed into a direct answer.

We have measured brands ranking first on Google that are entirely absent from the AI answer to the same query. This is a new discipline.

Search engine optimisation optimises for a list of blue links. Answer Engine Optimisation optimises for the generated answer. The goal is share of answer — not share of clicks.

03. The 5C Framework

Five dimensions. One system.

We structure AEO around five interconnected dimensions. Each feeds the others — you cannot fix citation without addressing structure, or structure without addressing signal authority.

C1
Clarity
Does the model understand what you are saying? We restructure content so the model can extract the answer directly — one question per page, concise answers, clear entity relationships marked up in schema.
C2
Authority
Models cite sources other sources cite. We map which outlets, research papers, forums, and data sets carry authority in your category, then build a prioritised plan for earning citations there.
C3
Structure
Schema, headings, entity relationships — the scaffolding that tells a model what a page is about. Without it, even good content gets ignored because the model cannot categorise it reliably.
C4
Freshness
Models weight recent content differently. A page from 2021 is nearly invisible even if still correct. We build content programmes that keep the model's view of your category updated.
C5
Measurement
Share of answer across models. Whether you are named, at what position, in response to which prompts — tracked month over month against a fixed, published baseline.

06. Honest limitations

What AEO cannot do.

We believe in AEO. But we also believe in telling you what it is not, so you can invest with your eyes open.

01
It cannot replace SEO.
AI search is growing fast, but traditional search remains the larger channel for most categories. AEO should sit alongside SEO, not replace it. If a brand ignores organic entirely in favour of AEO, they miss the majority of current demand.
02
It cannot guarantee citation.
AI models are black boxes. We can optimise the signals models are known to favour, but no agency can guarantee a specific citation outcome. Models change, retrieval algorithms shift, and what works today may work differently tomorrow. We track this honestly.
03
It cannot work without substance.
AEO is not a trick. Models are increasingly good at detecting content written solely for algorithmic consumption. The brands that win are the ones with real expertise, real data, and real authority to back up the answers they are cited for.
