Where the work appears

Each surface works differently. The optimisation must match.

Google Search
Traditional organic rankings, rich snippets, shopping results. Still the largest discovery channel for commercial intent. Crawl-based, link-weighted, keyword-matched.
AI Overviews
Embedded in SERP for informational queries. Selects and cites sources for structured summaries. High visibility at the awareness stage. Requires clear page structure, schema and authority signals that Google's retrieval system trusts.
AI Mode
Standalone conversational search inside Google. Handles multi-step queries, deep topic exploration and transactional shopping panels. Covers awareness through conversion. Content must support multi-step reasoning and product-level data.
ChatGPT
Conversational research with citations. Strong for awareness and consideration queries. Uses browsing and retrieval; citations depend on source authority, recency and semantic relevance. Shopping features emerging. Optimisation targets prompt alignment and citation-weighted sources.
Perplexity
Research-led answers with visible source selection. Strong for consideration-stage queries where users compare options. Favours authoritative, well-structured content and clear entity relationships. Source transparency makes gaps easy to identify.
Gemini
Integrated across Google Workspace, Android and Lens. Strong for awareness queries and image-based search. Multimodal retrieval means optimisation must cover text, structured data and visual content. Citations weighted toward Google's index and trusted sources.
Copilot
Embedded across Microsoft 365, Edge and Bing. High usage for B2B research across the full funnel. Retrieval influenced by Bing rankings and enterprise data. Optimisation targets professional queries, solution-oriented content and structured entity data.
Claude
Conversational with strong research capabilities. Used for deep topic exploration and comparison. Favours well-structured, authoritative content with clear entity definitions. Limited browsing means training data and cached retrieval matter more.

These systems do not return identical answers. Domain overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity is only 11%. The Search Intelligence baseline keeps every platform separate before any combined view is created.

The shift in search

The old search model is still useful. It is no longer complete.

The familiar model

Rank, click, visit.

Traditional SEO focuses on crawlability, rankings, organic traffic, technical health, content and links. Those foundations still matter.

The expanded model

Appear, get cited, get chosen.

AI systems generate answers, select sources and describe brands before a visitor reaches a website. Visibility now includes mention, recommendation, citation, source quality and the accuracy of the description.

The old measurement

Keyword position as the proxy.

A ranking report can show where a page sits in a search result. It cannot show whether a brand is absent from an AI answer to the same question.

The Inflexion measurement

A fixed prompt set and a source map.

We track the questions buyers ask, the models that answer them, the brands named, the position of each mention and the sources that support the answer.

Questions

How the programme fits together.

Why combine organic and AI search?

The systems overlap in their foundations but not in every signal. Technical access, clear content and entity structure support both. AI search also depends on source authority, retrieval patterns and the way a model assembles an answer. One programme prevents Inflexion from fixing one layer while ignoring the other.

What does Search Intelligence measure?

Inflexion defines a buyer prompt set, runs it across the models relevant to your category, and records mentions, recommendations, position, citations, sources and competitor presence. The baseline is fixed and published, so movement has a consistent denominator.

Does Digital PR mean buying links?

No. The source map identifies the publications, research, forums and data sources models already cite. Inflexion targets useful independent coverage and accurate entity descriptions. Paid or advertorial coverage represents only a small share of the citation data cited on this page.

Can technical SEO make a brand visible in AI answers?

It can remove an access problem, but it cannot create authority on its own. If the model cannot read the content, technical work is necessary. If the proposition is unclear or independent sources do not corroborate it, technical work is not sufficient.

How quickly should we expect movement?

Technical and content changes depend on crawling and retrieval schedules. Earned coverage may take weeks to appear in model outputs, and single placements rarely move the number alone. Inflexion sets a baseline first, then tracks repeated samples rather than treating one changed answer as proof.
