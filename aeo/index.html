The shift in search

Search became answers. Your SEO needs to follow.

Search engines return a list of blue links. Answer engines generate a direct response. The useful measure is no longer share of clicks alone. It is whether your brand appears in the answer, at what position, and in what context.

Organic & AI Search is not rebadged SEO. Keyword density matters less than factual clarity. Backlink volume matters less than citation frequency across authoritative sources. Page speed still matters, but so does whether an AI crawler can parse your content, verify your entities and retrieve a direct answer.

We have measured brands ranking first on Google that are entirely absent from the AI answer to the same query. The gap is commercial. Without a baseline, teams cannot tell whether they are gaining ground, losing ground, or reacting to a change in model output.

02 / STRATEGY

Organic & AI Search Strategy

Build the foundations that allow search engines and AI systems to understand the same proposition. Technical SEO, server-rendered content, schema, entity mapping, clear page structure and content designed for extraction.

A single page that directly answers one buyer question, with the right entity signals and source authority, can outperform dozens of pages written for keyword variation.

03 / AUTHORITY

Digital PR & Citations

AI models trust what others say about you more than what you say about yourself. We map the outlets, research papers, forums and data sets that carry weight in your category, then target the specific sources models already cite.

Consistent third-party descriptions reinforce the entity graph. A mention in a specialist publication may be more useful than several generic links from a syndicated wire.

Questions

How the programme fits together.

Why combine organic and AI search?

The systems overlap in their foundations but not in every signal. Technical access, clear content and entity structure support both. AI search also depends on source authority, retrieval patterns and the way a model assembles an answer. One programme prevents teams from fixing one layer while ignoring the other.

What does Search Intelligence measure?

We define a buyer prompt set, run it across the models relevant to your category, and record mentions, recommendations, position, citations, sources and competitor presence. The baseline is fixed and published, so month-to-month movement has a consistent denominator.

Does Digital PR mean buying links?

No. The source map identifies the publications, research, forums and data sources models already cite. Outreach is aimed at useful independent coverage and accurate entity descriptions. Paid or advertorial coverage represents only a small share of the citation data cited on this page.

Can technical SEO make a brand visible in AI answers?

It can remove an access problem, but it cannot create authority on its own. If the model cannot read the content, technical work is necessary. If the proposition is unclear or independent sources do not corroborate it, technical work is not sufficient.

How quickly should we expect movement?

Technical and content changes depend on crawling and retrieval schedules. Earned coverage may take weeks to appear in model outputs, and single placements rarely move the number alone. We set a baseline first, then track repeated samples rather than treating one changed answer as proof.
